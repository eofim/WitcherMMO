## Modification notice

WitcherMMO is not a simple redistribution of WitcherOnline. It is intended to contain substantial modifications focused on persistent multiplayer/MMO-oriented systems.

Planned areas of modification include, but are not limited to:

- custom character identity and creation;
- account and character persistence;
- server-side persistence;
- network entity ownership and replication;
- NPC and monster synchronization;
- shared combat state;
- shared quest/world state;
- MMO-oriented social, economy and scaling systems.

Unless explicitly documented otherwise, those planned systems should not be attributed to the original WitcherOnline authors.

Likewise, bugs introduced by WitcherMMO modifications should not be attributed to WitcherOnline or its contributors.
